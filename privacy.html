Privacy policy

Last updated: 7 November 2026

The short version. Kip keeps your training on your phone. If you create an account, it is also saved in our database so you can get it back. We never sell your data and we don't show ads. You can delete your account and everything in it from the app at any time.

Who we are

Kip: Calisthenics Coach ("Kip", "we", "us") is an app that helps you train toward calisthenics skills. For any privacy question or request, email feedback@kipcoach.app.

What we collect and why

WhatWhy
Account email (and your password, which is stored scrambled; we can never read it) To sign you in, keep your data safe and let you reset your password.
Profile: name, age, gender, height, weight (and changes to it), training goal, dream skill, experience, your self-test results (push-ups, pull-ups, plank), equipment, how often you want to train, any limitations or injuries you type in, and how you heard about Kip To build your plan: where you start on the skill map, which exercises fit your equipment, and what to be careful with.
Training data: workouts, sets, reps, hold times, added weight, how a workout felt, skill progress, unlock tests, personal records, your saved routines, and settings such as reminders and units To track your progress, choose your next workout and show your history.
Feedback: the type (bug, idea, other), your message, an optional screenshot, and the app version, phone model and OS version To read what you tell us and fix problems.
Crash reports (through Sentry): what went wrong in the app, the app version, phone model and OS version, and your account's random user ID To find and fix crashes. They never include your email, name or workout notes.
Basic usage events (see below) To understand which parts of Kip are used, so we can improve the app.

Your limitations, weight and other body details can be sensitive. You decide what to enter, and you can change or clear them in Settings at any time.

Usage events

Kip records a small number of usage events to improve the app: onboarding completed, account created, workout started, finished or cancelled, skill test passed or failed, the Kip Pro page viewed, and feedback sent. Each event has only small details such as the kind of workout, its length in minutes, the number of sets, or the skill and level tested, plus the app version and the time. They are linked to your account's random user ID if you have an account, and to nothing if you don't. They never contain your name, email or anything you type. We don't use any third-party analytics or advertising tools.

Without an account

You can use Kip without an account. Your profile and training then stay only on your phone. Crash reports, usage events and any feedback you send still reach us, without any account ID.

Where your data is kept

  • On your phone, in the app's private storage.
  • Supabase (our database and file storage) if you have an account, and for feedback and usage events. Every table is locked so each person can only reach their own data. Feedback screenshots are kept in a private storage area with no public links.
  • Sentry (crash reporting), only for crash reports. Sentry keeps them for a limited time (no more than 90 days).

These providers process data for us under their own security and privacy terms, and they may store it on servers outside your country. We don't share your data with anyone else, except when the law requires it.

We never sell your data

We don't sell, rent or trade your personal data, and we don't use it for advertising.

How long we keep it

Account, profile, training data, feedback and usage events: until you delete your account. Crash reports: up to 90 days. Data on your phone: until you sign out, reset the app or uninstall it.

How to delete your data

  • With an account: open Settings → Delete my account and type DELETE. This deletes your account, profile, training data, feedback, screenshots and the usage events linked to your account from our database straight away, then clears the phone.
  • Without an account: uninstalling Kip removes everything on your phone.
  • Can't open the app? Email feedback@kipcoach.app from your account email and we will delete it for you.

Your rights

In India, under the Digital Personal Data Protection Act, 2023, you can ask for a summary of the personal data we hold about you, have it corrected, completed or deleted, withdraw your consent at any time (withdrawing doesn't affect what happened before), nominate someone to act for you, and raise a grievance with us. We will answer within 30 days. If you're not happy with our answer, you can complain to the Data Protection Board of India.

Everywhere else, including the EU, UK and US states such as California, you have similar rights: to see your data, correct it, delete it, take a copy, and object to how we use it. You can also complain to your local data protection authority. We use your data because you chose to use Kip and gave it to us (consent and providing the service you asked for).

To use any of these rights, email feedback@kipcoach.app. This address also reaches our grievance contact.

Children

Kip is meant for people aged 18 and over. We don't knowingly collect data from children. If you believe a child has given us data, email us and we will delete it.

Security

Data travels encrypted (HTTPS). Database access is limited so each account reaches only its own rows. No system is perfectly secure, so please use a password you don't use anywhere else.

Fitness guidance, not medical advice

Kip gives general fitness guidance, not medical advice. Check with a doctor before starting a new training plan, especially if you have an injury or a health condition.

Changes to this policy

If we change this policy, we'll update the date at the top. If a change is important, we'll tell you in the app before it applies.
